Season FPT × how soft this round's opponent is at the player's position (per-36 allowed ÷ league). Players with 15+ min and 2+ games.

How it's calculated

  • Source: every player's game log from the EuroGuru API (the same FPT as the app), grouped by the opponent and the player's position (G/F/C).
  • Per 36 min is the fairer view: FPT allowed to a position ÷ minutes played at that position × 36. Per game is the raw total, which also depends on how many players of each position the opponents use.
  • Small sample: only games per team so far. It also reflects which opponents a team has faced. Treat it as a tiebreaker, not gospel. It gets more reliable each round.
  • Projection is a simple multiplier, not the EuroGuru model. It isn't a forecast; it shows the size of the matchup effect.
